Jin MinseoProduct Designer

I observe the behaviors users take for granted and dig deeper to understand why they repeat them. By bringing together interview insights and data, I uncover the root causes beneath the friction users experience.

I design services that take on the work users once had to calculate, remember, or figure out themselves. I reduce repetitive inputs and unnecessary steps, keeping only the information users need to make decisions. I shape experiences by removing more than I add.

By observing how people actually used a product after launch and refining the experience, I increased retention by 2.2×. I identify where engagement drops and users leave, investigate the causes, and keep refining the experience to give people a reason to return.
